We’re building infrastructure that helps your health data follow you wherever you receive care. Whether you move between departments in the same hospital or visit a different clinic, the people caring for you can access the medical history they need, with your permission.

That’s what continuity of care means to us. The next clinician can build on what’s already known about your health, and you spend less time piecing together old records.

We want to help people live longer. Making it easier for their care to continue across visits and providers is where we’re starting.
